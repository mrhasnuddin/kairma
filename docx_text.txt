The line is always live type — never baked into an AI image.The Western spine — one question, eight stationsKairma inherits a single question — what is the self when the body is made? — and the Western canon is eight stations on it. Plato sets reality behind a veil (the Cave): we see shadows. Aristotle answers the dualist temptation before it hardens — the soul is the form of the body, not a ghost lodged in it. Plotinus turns the gaze inward and makes the self a statue to be carved, not inflated. Aquinas christens the Aristotelian synthesis: the soul is the form of the body, known by its acts and its loves. Descartes splits the world in two with the cogito — and hands cyberpunk its central wound. Schopenhauer goes beneath the thinking self to the will that drives it. Freud dethrones the ego in its own house. Jung maps the unconscious and the persona — the mask — and warns that what we don’t make conscious we call fate.
