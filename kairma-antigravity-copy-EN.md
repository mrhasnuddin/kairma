### key: `s08_howitworks`
- heading: The flywheel that drives cost down with every deployment.
- steps:
  1. Source — We collect your operating SOPs.
  2. Encode — The SOP engine compiles them into executable agent workflows.
  3. Deploy — A forward-deployed team lands the workforce in your environment.
  4. Run — The workforce produces outcomes. You pay a subscription. Every token is spent on results, not idle usage.
  5. Compound — Each deployment standardizes the next. ~80% carries over; the last mile keeps shrinking.
